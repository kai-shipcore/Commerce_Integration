OOS Impact Analysis

Analyze the sales change around a Shopify Pre-Order conversion and the time required for marketplace sales to recover after restocking.

✅
This screen uses actual data. The Pre-Order SKU analysis and marketplace SKU recovery analysis read stockout and sales history from the database. The only exception is the Channel Comparison line chart on the marketplace tab, which still uses sample data and is labeled accordingly.

What each tab answers

① Shopify · Pre-Order Conversion Drop

Compare normal sales immediately before stockout with Pre-Order demand over an equal-length window. The analysis identifies both lost sales and products that sell more strongly while on Pre-Order.

② Marketplaces · Restock Recovery

Measure how many days Amazon, eBay, and Walmart products take to recover to 80% of their pre-stockout sales baseline after restocking.

1. Shopify · Pre-Order Conversion Drop

Shopify can continue accepting Pre-Orders while inventory is unavailable. The useful question is therefore not only whether sales became zero, but how the order rate changed compared with normal sales.

1

Select item, channel, and status

Multi-select Car Cover, Seat Cover, Floor Mat, or SWC and the Coverland B2C/B2B or Icarcover Shopify channels. Status options are All, Pre-Order Active, and Restocked.

2

Review reliable-analysis KPIs

KPIs, averages, medians, and the distribution include only episodes with at least 14 comparison days and a normal baseline of at least 1 unit per day.

3

Select a distribution range

Click a bar or its count to filter the detail grid to that range. Click the same bar again to clear the range filter.

4

Open an SKU episode

Click a row to open the comparison chart and its exact quantities, stockout date, restock date, and comparison window. On narrower screens, the detail panel is placed below the grid.

How is the comparison window selected?

  • The stockout start date is used as the Pre-Order conversion anchor.
  • The normal window ends immediately before the anchor; the Pre-Order window begins on the anchor.
  • The before and after windows always have the same length and are capped at 30 days.
  • If the item was restocked in fewer than 30 days, both sides use the actual Pre-Order duration. Example: restocked after 18 days → compare 18 days before with 18 days after.
  • The 30-day cap focuses on the immediate Pre-Order effect and limits contamination from longer-term seasonality, advertising, and promotions.

Drop-rate formula and negative values

Sales drop rate = (Normal daily average − Pre-Order daily average) ÷ Normal daily average × 100

Example: 10 units/day normally → 6 units/day on Pre-Order = 40% drop

Example: 1.28 units/day normally → 2.06 units/day on Pre-Order = -61% drop (61% sales increase)

📈
A negative drop rate is not an error. It means sales increased after the product moved to Pre-Order. The screen identifies these episodes with a blue Sales Increase badge and a separate histogram bucket. Strong demand during stockout may justify faster replenishment, substitution planning, or a higher purchase priority.

2. Marketplaces · Restock Recovery

For Amazon, eBay, and Walmart, an out-of-stock product normally records no sales. This tab measures whether and when sales recovered after inventory returned. Products that are still out of stock are excluded because post-restock recovery cannot yet be measured.

Core recovery rules

RuleDefinition
Pre-stockout baselineThe daily average during the 30 days immediately before the stockout began.
Recovery eventThe first day when the trailing 14-calendar-day daily average reaches at least 80% of the baseline. The elapsed days become Days to Recovery.
Minimum ageOnly episodes at least 7 days past restock are analyzed because very recent daily averages are too unstable.
Normal RecoveryReached the 80% threshold within 30 days.
Slow RecoveryReached the threshold after 30 days but within the 90-day horizon.
PendingHas not reached 80%, but fewer than 90 days have passed since restock.
Not RecoveredDid not reach 80% within 90 days after restock.

Using the recovery screen

  • Multi-select marketplace channels to change the analysis scope.
  • The Prior OOS 14/30/60-day choices are visible but are not wired to filtering yet. The current result uses All durations.
  • The SKU Comparison bars group episodes into 0–30 days, 30–60 days, 60–90 days, Pending, and Not Recovered. Click a bar or count to filter the grid.
  • Click a detail row to review the pre-stockout baseline, actual post-restock sales chart, and the non-overlapping 0–30, 30–60, and 60–90-day daily averages.
  • The Channel Comparison line chart still uses sample data. Use SKU Comparison and the row detail data for operational decisions.
ℹ️
Current functional scope: SKU Comparison, bar filtering, sorting, search, pagination, and row-level detail charts use actual data. The Prior OOS duration filter and actual Channel Comparison aggregation remain follow-up work.

Operational decisions

  • Purchase priority: review SKUs with the largest daily Pre-Order loss or unusually strong demand while out of stock.
  • Safety stock: products with both a large Pre-Order drop and slow post-restock recovery may need more protection inventory.
  • Marketplace follow-up: for Not Recovered products, check price, ads, search visibility, reviews, competing listings, and listing health.
  • Repeated stockouts: compare the stockout and actual restock dates for each episode when one SKU appears more than once.
⚠️
Interpretation caution: A before/after change does not prove that stockout or Pre-Order was the only cause. Review price, promotions, advertising, seasonality, competition, and listing visibility before making a final decision.
