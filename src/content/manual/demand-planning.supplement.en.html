⑫ OOS (90D) — Recent Stockout Duration and Lost Demand

OOS (Out of Stock) means a period when inventory was unavailable and sales opportunities may have been missed. This group shows how long each SKU was out of stock during the trailing 90 days and the estimated marketplace demand lost during those periods.

ColumnMeaningHow to Read It
OOS Period The number of days within the trailing 90-day window when available inventory was zero or below. 0 means no recorded stockout period in the trailing 90 days. A larger number means the SKU remained out of stock longer.
OOS Lost Demand The quantity estimated to have been missed on Amazon, eBay, and Walmart, using Shopify preorders received during the stockout as the baseline. This is not a count of actual canceled orders. It is an estimate based on channel sales ratios; a larger value suggests a greater potential sales impact from the stockout.

When is the data updated?

Run Sync in the top toolbar or Apply & Sync in Planning Settings. The system then recalculates the values as follows:

1

Read inventory history

The system finds each SKU's stockout and recovery dates from the trailing 120 days of inventory history. available > 0 is treated as in stock; all other values are treated as out of stock.

2

Calculate the trailing 90-day OOS period

Only the portion of each stockout episode that overlaps the trailing 90-day window is counted. An SKU that has not recovered is treated as remaining out of stock through today.

3

Estimate lost demand

Shopify preorders received during the stockout and category-level channel ratios are used to estimate expected Amazon, eBay, and Walmart sales. Actual marketplace sales during the same period are then subtracted.

🧮
Simple example: Suppose a stockout period has 10 Shopify preorders and the category's Amazon ratio is 300% of Shopify. Expected Amazon sales are 30 units. If 5 units were actually sold on Amazon during the same period, Amazon lost demand is max(0, 10 × 3 − 5) = 25 units. eBay and Walmart are calculated the same way and then added together.

OOS Lost-Demand Marketplace Weights

Open ⚙ Planning Settings in the top toolbar to review or change the Amazon, eBay, and Walmart weights for each product category.

SettingBehavior
Auto This is the default. For Seat Cover (SC), Car Cover (CC), and Floor Mat (FM), each marketplace ratio is recalculated on every Sync from trailing 90-day sales as marketplace sales ÷ Shopify sales. Auto values appear in light gray.
Manual value The percentage you enter replaces the auto value. For example, entering 300% for Amazon means three units of expected Amazon demand for each Shopify preorder.
Restore to Auto Clears manual overrides and returns to ratios calculated from recent sales data.
Apply & Sync Saves the current settings, recalculates OOS duration and lost demand, and updates the grid.
⚠️
OOS Lost Demand is an estimate. It uses inventory history, Shopify preorders, and recent channel ratios. It does not fully reproduce changes in advertising, pricing, seasonality, or marketplace exposure. Use it together with actual sales trends when making purchase decisions.
💡
If the columns are hidden: Open ⊞ Columns ▾, enable OOS Period and OOS Lost Demand under Column Visibility → OOS (90D), then click Apply at the bottom.

⑬ Formula Columns — Your Own Formulas per Cell

Formula columns are columns where you enter a formula in each cell. Put a formula in each cell and fill it down to apply the same calculation to many SKUs. Results are calculated only in this tab; order quantities, stock, and purchasing data are not changed.

TaskHow
Open formula columns Click Formula columns in the top toolbar to open the formula editor. Click + Add formula column to create a new column. It starts empty, and values appear only in cells where you enter a formula.
Rename a column Edit Formula column name under Column settings in the formula editor. The name is saved when you leave the field.
Enter a formula Click a cell in the formula column, type a formula starting with = in the formula field, then click Apply or press Enter. If you select several cells first, the same formula goes into all of them.
Type directly in a cell Double-click a cell, or select it and press F2, to edit the formula inside the cell. Enter applies the change and moves to the cell below; Esc cancels.
Fill down Select two or more rows in the same formula column, then click Fill down or press Ctrl+D. The formula in the top cell is copied to the cells below. [columnID] refers to the row of each copied cell.
Reference another cell While editing a formula, click Pick cell and then click a cell in the grid to insert it as a reference. The address of the referenced cell (for example, C6) appears to the left of the formula field. To use another column in the same row, pick it from the Insert column from this row… list.
Clear cells Use Clear selected cells to erase formulas in the selected cells. The ✕ next to the formula field only resets the formula you are typing.

Formula Rules

ItemRule
Same-SKU value [columnID] — refers to a value in the same row (the same SKU).
Specific-SKU value SKU("SKU", "columnID") — refers to a value in the row of the specified SKU.

Functions IF, IFERROR, MIN, MAX, SUM, ABS, ROUND, ROUNDUP, ROUNDDOWN
Length A formula can be up to 2000 characters.

⑭ As Of Date — Replaying a Past Date

The date input at the top is the reference date. When you choose a past date, sales and stock are recalculated as of that date. S.O.D. and inventory life are also recalculated from that date. Use it to check which SKUs were at risk last week, or why a stock decision looked the way it did at that time.

ItemOn a past reference date
Sales (90/60/30/15/7-day, averages) Re-aggregated for the chosen date.
Total Stock, Back Restored from stock history to that day's state.
Warehouse stock (Fullerton, Canary, TTM, West, East) Shown as —. Stock history keeps only SKU totals, so it cannot be split by warehouse.
Transit, Remaining / Mistake, container list, ETA, and quantities No history is kept, so the current values are used.

In other words, a past reference date shows "that day's sales and stock × the current transit and container plan". The note next to the date shows the stock reference date and which items are current. Hover over it for the full explanation.

📅
Selectable date range: You can only choose dates that the sales and inventory history can fully answer. Earlier dates are blocked in the date picker, and hovering over the input shows the earliest selectable date. Use the Today button to return to today.
⚠️
A past reference date is not a full snapshot of that time. Transit stock and the container plan are current values, so to reconstruct a past decision, check the container status from the time of the order separately.
