Sales Velocity

Compare how many units sold by product, channel, mode, and time period.

💡
Important: 90D, 30D, and 7D are total units sold in each period, not units per day. Divide by the number of days or compare short and long windows to understand the pace of demand.

Quick Start — 3 Steps

① Choose a product

Select Car Cover, Seat Cover, Floor Mat, or SWC.

② Choose channels and mode

Select the sales channels, then choose Sales, TTM, or Pre-Order.

③ Compare periods

Use 90D·60D·30D·15D·7D or enter exact date ranges.

Understanding Modes

ModeIncluded dataBest use
SalesOrders classified as regular salesReview recent demand for inventory planning
TTMOrders classified as TTMCompare TTM demand separately from regular sales
Pre-OrderPreorders and, where applicable, TTM preordersReview reserved demand not treated as regular sales
⚠️
Avoid accidental double counting: Sales, TTM, and Pre-Order are different classifications. Do not add them together without confirming the business meaning.

Setting Date Periods

Period mode

  • The defaults are 90D, 60D, 30D, 15D, and 7D.
  • Click a number to edit it, use × to remove it, or use Add for another period.
  • Keep between one and five periods. The reset arrow restores the defaults.

Custom mode

Enter exact start and end dates. Both dates are included, and the inclusive day count appears beside the range. This is useful for promotions, month-to-month comparisons, or other fixed business periods.

📅
Two-day reporting delay: Default rolling windows end at today minus two days for data stability. If today is July 22, the default 7D window is July 14–20.

Reading the Table

ElementMeaning
Master SKUThe master SKU used to aggregate sales.
90D / 30D / 7DTotal units sold inside each period. Windows overlap, so do not add them together.
Total rowThe period total for all matching SKUs. It remains at the top while searching or sorting.
0No units sold in that period. Rows with zero across every visible section are hidden.
-No Link, Custom, or TTM ranked record is available in that display position.

Simple trend example

If a SKU shows 90D=900, 30D=360, and 7D=105, the approximate daily averages are 10, 12, and 15 units. Because the recent 7-day pace is above the 90-day pace, demand appears to be accelerating. Lower short-window averages may indicate slowing demand.

Search, Sort, and Pagination

  • Search: Enter any part of a Master SKU. The Total row stays visible.
  • Sort: Click Master SKU or a Link/Total period header to toggle ascending and descending order.
  • Pages: The default is 100 rows per page. Use the controls below the table to change pages or page size.

Sync and Export

ActionWhat it does
SyncReloads source orders and refreshes internal Velocity snapshots. The button is disabled during processing, and Last synced updates afterward.
ExportDownloads all data for the current product, channels, mode, and periods to Excel.
Export AllDownloads Sales, TTM, and Pre-Order side by side in one worksheet using the same product, channels, and periods.

Troubleshooting

SymptomWhat to check
No tableMake sure at least one channel is selected.
Expected order is missingCheck product, channel, mode, period, and UTC/LA basis. Default windows exclude the latest two days.
No Custom columnsCustom columns are shown only for Seat Cover.
Data looks oldCheck Last synced and run Sync if you have permission.
Sync already in progressAnother user may be syncing. Wait briefly and try again.
Date totals differTotals use the UTC order date, so an order placed after 5pm Los Angeles time counts on the next day.
