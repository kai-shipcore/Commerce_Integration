Demand Planning (Planning Dashboard)

The Demand Planning dashboard brings SKU sales, inventory, inbound schedules, and container order planning into one grid. You can select headers, entire columns, or cells and apply spreadsheet-style sorting, filtering, hiding, colors, and text formatting. Custom header names, column order, widths, visibility, and formatting are saved separately for each user.

ℹ️
Important: A single click on a column header no longer sorts the grid. It selects the header. Right-click the header to sort, filter, or hide the column.
▲ Demand Planning combines SKU rows, pinned information, sales and inventory metrics, and container planning columns.

First Visit — Five-Step Quick Start

1

Choose the scope

Select category, product, urgency, and As of date. Search for an SKU when needed.

2

Show only useful columns

Open ⊞ Columns ▾, choose a preset or individual checkboxes, and click Apply.

3

Narrow the rows

Right-click a header and choose Sort or Filter. You can also record workflow states in Note and filter by those states.

4

Review and edit

Edit permitted values such as CBM, Note, ETA, and Con. Qty, then review the recalculated results.

5

Save pending quantities

After automatic or manual quantity changes, review the pending state and click Save.

③ Sort, Filter, and Hide Columns

Right-click a base-column or container sub-column header to open its menu.

Menu ItemBehavior
Sort A → ZSorts text alphabetically and numbers or dates from lower to higher values.
Sort Z → ASorts text in reverse order and numbers or dates from higher to lower values.
FilterChoose Filter by values or Filter by condition. Click Apply to commit the staged filter; Cancel preserves the current filter.
Hide columnHides the column. If the right-clicked column belongs to the current multi-selection, all columns represented by the selected headers, entire columns, or cells are hidden together.

Filter examples

  • Filter by values: Search the distinct-value list and check only values to display. (blank) is available as a value.
  • Filter by condition: Use empty/not empty; text contains, does not contain, starts with, ends with, or equals; or numeric greater than, at least, less than, at most, equals, not equal, between, or not between.
  • Clear a filter: Select all values, or set the condition to None, and click Apply.
↩️
Restore hidden columns: A restore arrow is attached to the nearest real header at the hidden position. Hover to see hidden names, then click to restore the contiguous hidden run. You can also enable columns again under Column Visibility.

⑧ Container Planning and Auto Calculation

Each container can show planning sub-columns such as Con. Qty, remaining inventory, open orders, available quantity, estimated sales, backorder, carryover, Inv. Life, EST. SOD, Plan SOD, and CBM. Some are inputs; others are chained results based on the previous container and ETA.

FeatureDescription
Fixed target 📅Uses the target days in the header—90 by default—to calculate a Con. Qty draft for each SKU.
Tiered target 📊Applies different target days to different daily-sales tiers.
PreviewCalculated values appear in the grid immediately but remain a draft. Review quantity, total CBM, and capacity warnings.
Save / ResetSave commits pending quantities. Reset returns to the last saved values after a confirmation prompt.
Container detailsUse the container-header navigation action to open that container's planning details in a new tab.
